Everything you need to know before deploying your first AI employee.

Most customers are live in under 15 minutes. Connect your tools using our one-click integrations, upload any existing knowledge base or FAQ documents, and deploy. Our onboarding wizard guides you through every step. There's no coding, no API keys to configure manually, and no lengthy setup calls required — though our team is always available if you want them.

NeuraFlow trains on your brand voice, tone guidelines, past conversations, and product knowledge — so your AI agents respond the way your best employee would, not like a generic chatbot. You can define personality traits, communication style, escalation rules, and even specific phrases to use or avoid. Most customers tell us customers can't tell they're talking to an AI.

You control escalation behaviour completely. The AI can: seamlessly hand off to a human with full conversation context attached, send a "we'll follow up shortly" reply and create a ticket, or ask a clarifying question to gather more information. Every handoff is logged, and the AI learns from how your team resolves edge cases — so it handles them autonomously next time.

You'll never hit a hard wall or have your AI go offline. We alert you at 80% usage so you can upgrade or purchase a top-up bundle. Overage is billed at a transparent per-interaction rate shown clearly in your dashboard. Enterprise customers get soft limits and a dedicated account manager who monitors usage proactively — no surprise bills, ever.

Security is foundational — not an add-on. NeuraFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA-ready for healthcare customers on Enterprise plans. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Your data is never used to train shared models. You can also request data residency in the US, EU, or APAC regions, and full data deletion at any time.
